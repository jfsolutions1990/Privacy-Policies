Privacy Policy

SnapBond (package com.snapbond.app)
Effective date: 27 August 2026 · Last updated: 27 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the SnapBond mobile application ("SnapBond", the "App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy.

SnapBond is a messaging‑assistance utility that helps you write opening messages, replies, and date invitations for dating and social conversations. The App has no user accounts and never displays advertisements to you. It does include third‑party measurement tools used solely to determine which of our own advertising campaigns lead to installs and subscriptions — see section 2.6. The content you type is never shared with those tools.

2. Information we collect

We designed SnapBond to collect as little as possible. We do not require you to create an account or sign in, and we do not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifiers. A first name you enter during setup is stored only on your device (see 2.4). Because SnapBond helps with dating and social messaging, the text you choose to enter to generate suggestions can reveal information about your relationships or social life; that text is handled as described in 2.1.

2.1 Content you provide (to generate suggestions)

When you use the App's tools, you type in information that is needed to generate suggestions — for example a match's first name, a few "details" about them, or messages from a conversation you paste in. This text is sent to our processing service and to our AI provider solely to generate the requested openers, replies, or invitations, and the generated results are returned to your device.

  • We do not use this content to build a profile of you.
  • We do not sell this content.
  • This content is not used to train AI models.
  • Our processing service does not store the text of your inputs after your request is completed (see Section 6).

Please avoid entering highly sensitive personal information (such as full names, addresses, financial details, or government identifiers). Our AI prompts are designed to avoid repeating such details, but you control what you type.

2.2 Device identifier (free‑trial and abuse prevention)

To enforce the free‑trial usage limit and prevent abuse of our AI service, the App sends a device identifier — the Android ANDROID_ID value provided by your device — to our processing service, where it is used to count how many free generations have been made from your device. No content from your messages is stored alongside this counter.

2.3 Purchases and subscriptions

SnapBond offers an optional paid subscription ("SnapBond Pro") through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed by Google. We do not receive or store your credit‑card number or full payment details. We receive from Google only the information needed to confirm and maintain your subscription entitlement.

2.4 Information stored only on your device

The following are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us:

  • The first name you enter during setup (used only to personalize greetings inside the App).
  • Your settings and preferences (tone, intent, boldness, momentum threshold, privacy mode, app‑lock).
  • Your history of generated results and the inputs associated with them (unless Privacy Mode is on, in which case results are not saved).
  • Your subscription status flag.

This local data is removed when you use "Delete History" (for history) or when you uninstall the App (the App is configured so uninstalling clears its stored data).

2.6 Advertising measurement and analytics

SnapBond does not show you any advertisements. However, because we advertise the App on other platforms, we use analytics tools to measure whether those ads work. To do this, the App shares your device's Advertising ID and a small number of app-activity events with the advertising platforms listed in section 4.

The events we send are limited to coarse milestones:

  • You finished the setup flow.
  • You generated suggestions for the first time.
  • You viewed the subscription screen.
  • You purchased a subscription, together with its price and currency.

We never send the content you type or receive. The names, details, conversations, messages you paste, and the suggestions generated for you are never included in any analytics or advertising event. Advertising platforms receive only the milestone names above and, for a purchase, the amount paid.

You can limit this at any time in your Android settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can reset or delete your Advertising ID. Deleting it stops the App from sharing a usable advertising identifier.

6. Data retention

  • Message/generation content: transmitted only to fulfil your request and is not retained by our processing service after the request completes. Any transient processing or short‑term operational logging by our AI provider is governed by that provider's policies.
  • Free‑trial counter (device identifier): retained by our processing service for as long as needed to enforce trial limits and prevent abuse.
  • Local data (settings and history): kept on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Subscription data: retained by Google and by us only as needed to maintain your entitlement and meet legal/accounting obligations.

7. Data security

Data sent between the App and our processing service is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to our service is restricted with a shared application token. History and settings on your device can be additionally protected with the optional biometric App Lock. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
